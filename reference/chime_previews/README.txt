REFERENCE-ONLY RECONSTRUCTED CHIME PREVIEWS

These WAV files are generated from the same constants/envelope used by the GE runtime.
They are NOT extracted Ford/OEM audio assets and are NOT required by the application.
The production 2020 Escape IPC firmware exposes chime commands/types and normally asks
the ACM/DSP to render warning audio; no recoverable WAV/Ogg chime asset was found in
the supplied LV4T-14C088/14C026 cluster payloads.
